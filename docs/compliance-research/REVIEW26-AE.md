# United Arab Emirates: what was done with the round-26 independent audit (questions and coverage)

Done on 2026-11-13. "Structural" means a question's wording, order, help, optional flag or who it is put to was changed,
or a condition was made to agree with the item's own words: no page needed. "Fact" means the official page was opened
in this session with WebFetch (PDFs were saved by WebFetch and read as text) and the sentence is quoted. Every FIXED line
is held by `tests/verify/review26_ae.ts` (31 checks, fixed "today" 2026-11-13). Base pack `ae.ts` 2026.11.3 -> 2026.11.4
(the version-ledger failure in `compliance_engine.ts` is expected; `--record-versions` was not run). New research file
`ae-r26.json` is loaded last: `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/compliance-import.ts $D/ae.json $D/ae-fixes.json $D/ae-gaps.json $D/ae-r26.json --write`
(0 left out).

The Federal Tax Authority marks every English text of the tax laws used here as unofficial: the VAT Executive
Regulation and the excise Executive Regulation open with "This is not an official translation", and the penalties
decision with "This is not an official Translation". The two new tax items say so in their `note`.

## What the engine could not express
- A choice's options cannot depend on the emirate: `ae_x_fz_zone` lists the Dubai zones and RAKEZ together (each item also tests the emirate, so a wrong pick shows nothing wrong).
- A choose-several question has no "Not sure" in the checker, which is why the AML questions were not merged (M11).
- The grace period depends on the visa category, which no question asks; only the shortest figure read is shown.
- Service Master's plain-words description of an `anniversary` rule with `first: true` still reads "The anniversary of ..." (describe.ts, not edited).
