# Canada: what was done with the independent review's findings (C1 to C17)

Corrected on 2026-11-12. "Structural" means the rule was made to agree with the item's own words; "fact" means an
official page was opened on that day and the sentence it rests on is quoted. Every fixed finding is held by
`tests/verify/review_ca.ts` (42 checks, fixed "today" 2026-11-11). Base pack `ca.ts` went from version 2026.11.4 to 2026.11.5.

| Finding | Result | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | FIXED for Québec, Alberta, Ontario and Newfoundland and Labrador (fact); NOT DONE for the other provinces | A federal corporation was shown nothing provincial. **Québec**: `ca_ar_qc` now also applies to federal corporations. quebec.ca, Annual updating declaration: "All enterprises registered in the enterprise register are required to file an annual updating declaration during the prescribed period, even if they have no changes to make"; legal persons "May 15 to November 15". **Alberta**: new `ca_ar_ab_xp`, counted from a new date question `ca_xp_reg_date` (date of registration in the province). Business Corporations Act, current as of September 2, 2026 (https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/documents/Acts/B09.pdf), s. 292(1): "A registered extra-provincial corporation shall, in each year on or before the last day of the month immediately following its anniversary month, send to the Registrar a return"; s. 276(a): anniversary month "means the month in each year that is the same as the month in which its certificate of registration was issued"; s. 1(r): an extra-provincial corporation is "a body corporate incorporated otherwise than by or under an Act of the Legislature"; alberta.ca: "If your corporation is from another province or country, you will have to file annual returns in Alberta." **Ontario**: new `ca_x_on_ep_initial`, and `ca_x_on_change` no longer excludes federal corporations. Notice on00231e, effective October 19, 2021 (https://forms.mgcs.gov.on.ca/dataset/963f3b12-ce06-488a-a4e2-cb245293708e/resource/37f707f8-b863-4b18-9c29-e408df6472f7/download/on00231e.pdf): "Extra-provincial corporations must file an Initial Return under the Corporations Information Act (CIA) ... within 60 days after the date the corporation begins to carry on business in Ontario (section 3 of the CIA) ... a Notice of Change ... within 15 days after the change takes place"; "CIA filing requirements apply to all corporations with or without share capital wherever or however incorporated and includes an extra-provincial corporation". Ontario's yearly return stays excluded (its notice lists Ontario corporations and licensed foreign corporations only). **Newfoundland and Labrador**: new `ca_ar_nl_xp`, marked "to confirm". gov.nl.ca, Annual returns: "Form 28 is to be used by extra-provincial corporations carrying on business in the province"; "a corporation must file an Annual Return prior to the end of month in which the date of its registration". The page does not say in words that a federal corporation is an extra-provincial one, hence "to confirm". **Not done**: British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island and the territories. Their pages were either not opened or say nothing on it (BC's guide info_36 and Saskatchewan's extra-provincial page have no sentence on the yearly return of an extra-provincial corporation). A federal corporation there is still shown only the federal filings. |
| C2 | FIXED (fact) | `ca_ar_bc` now applies to companies only. New `ca_ar_bc_society` for an incorporated non-profit in British Columbia, counted from a new date question `ca_agm_date`. Societies Act (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/15018_01), s. 73(1): "A society must, within 30 days after an annual general meeting is held, file with the registrar an annual report"; s. 71(1): the directors "must call annual general meetings so that an annual general meeting is held in each calendar year." The rule for a year with no meeting (a statement by January 31 of the next year, s. 73(2)) was read in the tool's summary of the page, not as a quotation. |
| C3 | FIXED in part (fact) | New `ca_ar_sk_npo`. saskregistries.ca, Maintaining a non-profit corporation: "Each year, every non-profit corporation must send an annual return to the Corporate Registry"; the financial statement "is to be filed with the Corporate Registry not more than 30 days after the annual meeting at which it was presented." The page gives no due date for the annual return itself, so the date shown is the financial statement's (30 days after the meeting date given) and the words say the return's date comes on the registry's notice. |
| C4 | FIXED (fact) | New question `ca_gre_fy` ("Is it the estate of someone who died, with a tax year that does not end on December 31?") and date question `ca_gre_year_end`. Each trust filing now has a second entry for such an estate (`ca_t3_gre`, `ca_sched15_gre`, `ca_t3_slips_gre`, `ca_t1135_trust_gre`, `ca_t106_trust_gre`, `ca_t1141_trust_gre`, `ca_t1142_trust_gre`, `ca_nr4_trust_gre`), counted 90 days from the year end given; with no year end the date is "still needed". CRA, T3 return: when to file: "A graduated rate estate (GRE) can have a non-calendar tax year"; "All other trusts are generally required to use a December 31 tax year-end"; "The T3 return must be filed no later than 90 days after the trust's tax year-end." Also found while fixing: `ca_nr4` showed 31 March to a trust although its own words say 90 days after the year end (30 March in a leap year). Split into `ca_nr4` (not a trust) and `ca_nr4_trust`. |
| C5 | FIXED (structural) | `ca_x_on_disconnect`, `ca_x_on_emon`: fixed date 1 March changed to 28 February (the policy must be in place "before March 1"). In a leap year the 29th also counts; the 28th is the early side. |
| C6 | FIXED for Ontario (structural) and for British Columbia's pay transparency report (fact); NOT CHANGED for `ca_x_bc_jhsc` and `ca_x_qc_demes` | `ca_x_fedreg = yes` now excludes `ca_x_on_poster`, `_disconnect`, `_emon`, `_critical`, `_jhsc`, `_supervisor`, `_policy_review` (the question's own help and the poster's note say federally regulated workplaces follow the Canada Labour Code). `ca_x_bc_paytransparency` excluded on the BC page (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/about-the-bc-government/gender-equity/preparing-pay-transparency-reports): "A provincially regulated employer above a certain size must complete a pay transparency report by November 1 of each year"; "Requirements may differ for federally regulated employers." WorkSafeBC's joint committee page says nothing about federally regulated employers, so `ca_x_bc_jhsc` is unchanged. CNESST refused access (HTTP 403), so `ca_x_qc_demes` is unchanged in audience. The WSIB injury report (`ca_x_on_form7`) was not in the finding and is unchanged. |
| C7 | FIXED (structural, with one fact) | The sales-tax questions and items now also reach an individual who answered Yes to "Did you, or your spouse or common-law partner, carry on a business this year?" (`SELLER` in `ca.ts`): GST/HST registration, the yearly return on the sole proprietor's dates (30 April payment, 15 June filing), quarterly and monthly returns, instalments, and the provincial sales taxes. An individual with no business is asked nothing about sales tax. Because the question includes a spouse's business, the GST/HST question's help says "Answer for your own business". New question `ca_taxi` for sole proprietors and such individuals; Yes brings `ca_gst_register` whatever the sales. CRA, When to register (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): "If you are a self-employed taxi driver or commercial ride-sharing driver, you have to register for the GST/HST even if you are a small supplier." |
| C8 | FIXED (fact) | `ca_over30k` is now asked of businesses only; non-profits and charities get new `ca_over50k`. A charity over $50,000 whose gross revenue is $250,000 or less is no longer shown `ca_gst_a` (or asked how often it files) unless it says it is registered. CRA guide RC4081: "An NPO is a small supplier if the total amount of all revenues (before expenses) from the worldwide taxable supplies of all of the organization's activities and those of its associates, is $50,000 or less in any single calendar quarter and in the last four consecutive calendar quarters"; RC4022: "Public service body means a charity, non-profit organization, municipality, university, public college, school authority, or hospital authority"; When to register: charities "gross revenue ... $250,000 or less". |
| C9 | FIXED (fact) | `ca_pst_freq` replaced by `ca_pst_freq_bc` (monthly, quarterly, every six months, yearly), `ca_pst_freq_sk` and `ca_pst_freq_mb` (monthly, quarterly, yearly), each asked only of a seller in that province. New `ca_bc_pst_s` (every six months; the dates assume half-years ending 30 June and 31 December and the note says so). BC, Report and pay PST: "More than $3,000 up to $6,000: Quarterly or semi-annual"; "$3,000 or less: Quarterly, semi-annual or annual". Saskatchewan, File your PST return: annually "$0 to $4,800 per year", quarterly "$4,800 to $12,000 per year", monthly "over $12,000 per year". Manitoba Bulletin RST 004: "$5,000 or more – monthly; $500 to $4,999 – quarterly; Less than $500 – annually." |
| C10 | FIXED (fact) | `ca_t5013_c/_m/_i` now cite guide T4068 (opened; the dates agree). `ca_ar_ab` now cites Alberta's Business Corporations Regulation, s. 24(1) (https://kings-printer.alberta.ca/documents/Regs/2000_118.pdf): "The prescribed date for the purposes of sending an annual return to the Registrar is a date not later than the last day of the month immediately following the anniversary month." `ca_gst_instal` now cites the CRA page with the dates (pay-instalment/when-to-pay): "Instalment payments are due within one month after the end of each of your fiscal quarters." `ca_gst_a/_a_sp/_q/_m` carry `penaltySource: gst_pen` (GST/HST filing penalties): "Calculate: A + (B × C)": A "1% of the amount owing", B "25% of A", C "the number of complete months the return is overdue, to a maximum of 12 months". The thresholds in the help of `ca_gst_freq` were read in RC4022 and agree ("$1,500,000 or less: Annual"; "More than $1,500,000 up to $6,000,000: Quarterly"; "More than $6,000,000: Monthly"; "Charities are assigned an annual reporting period"); a question's help has no source field, so nothing was changed there. |
| C11 | FIXED (fact) | `ca_partners` has four answers: corporations only; a mix of corporations with individuals or trusts; individuals and trusts, or trusts only (new `trusts`); individuals only. The forms due "with the partnership return" (`ca_t1135`, `ca_t106`, `ca_t1141`, `ca_t1142`) now have a third entry `_pship_i` (31 March) for individuals and trusts; `_pship` (the earlier-of rule) is for mixed partnerships only. |

## Things to know
- New questions: `ca_gre_fy`, `ca_gre_year_end`, `ca_over50k`, `ca_taxi`, `ca_pst_freq_bc`, `ca_pst_freq_sk`,
  `ca_pst_freq_mb` (in place of `ca_pst_freq`), `ca_xp_reg_date`, `ca_agm_date`. An answer saved under `ca_pst_freq`
  is not read any more: the person is asked again.
- New items: `ca_ar_bc_society`, `ca_ar_sk_npo`, `ca_ar_ab_xp`, `ca_ar_nl_xp`, `ca_bc_pst_s`, `ca_nr4_trust`,
  the eight `_gre` entries, the four `_pship_i` entries, and `ca_x_on_ep_initial` (research file).
- New sources: `t4068`, `gst_pen`, `gst_instal_when`, `bc_soc`, `sk_npo`, `ab_bca`, `ab_bcr`, `ca_x_src_on_cia_ep`.
- `penaltySource` cannot be written inside the short form `C(...)` (it is not in `CDef` in `dsl.ts`), so `ca.ts` and
  `ae.ts` set it beside the call.
- Noticed, not changed: the words of `ca_ar_bc` end with "For a company from outside B.C.: within two months after the
  anniversary of its registration in B.C." That sentence is not in the guide the item cites (info_36, July 2023).
