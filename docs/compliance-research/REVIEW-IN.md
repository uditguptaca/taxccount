# India: what was done about the independent review (findings I1 to I31)

Worked on 2026-11-12 against pack `IN 2026.11.3`. Base pack now `2026.11.4` (`src/lib/compliance/countries/in.ts`);
researched items in `in.json`, `in-gaps.json`, corrections in `in-fixes.json`; `in-more.ts` re-written by the importer
with no rejected item. Every finding fixed is held by `tests/verify/review_in.ts` (52 checks).

**Kinds of correction.** STRUCTURAL = the rule, condition or label made to agree with the item's own words. FACT = what
the app says changed; the official page was opened in this session and the sentence is quoted here.

**How pages were opened.** `wm.incometaxindia.gov.in` and `incometax.gov.in` answered a plain request that names itself
(`compliance-source-check`); the same request through the page-summarising fetcher got 403, so that fetcher was not used
for them. India Code section texts were read from the site's own data address (`indiacode.gov.in/server/api/pid/find?id=…`).
Not opened, and not worked around: the Income-tax Rules, 2026 (index page 403), `taxinformation.cbic.gov.in` (connection
reset), `mca.gov.in`. Where a fact rests on one of those, the EARLIER date is shown and the item is "to confirm".

## Beyond the findings

- Two workplace-safety reports that had no penalty now carry the Code's general penalty with its page (https://indiacode.gov.in/handle/123456789/512367, s.94), worded as applying "by its own terms" like the board-meeting item.
