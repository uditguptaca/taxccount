## Questions

- B2 in_senior exemption ignores residence: FIXED. The question is now `in_senior60` ("Are you 60 or older?"), asked only when `in_resident` is yes; advance tax (`in_advance`) is excused only for senior AND resident AND no business income. The auditor's case (non-resident, 60+) now gets advance tax.
- B3 in_profession either/or: FIXED. Replaced by the choice `in_biz_kind` (business / profession / both, new id). "Both" is asked turnover AND professional receipts, and either limit triggers the tax audit; profession-only is not asked turnover, business-only not asked receipts.
- C6 help for legal-conclusion questions: FIXED (plain words, no new figures): `in_x_g_re` (how to tell; Principal Officer is from the FIU items' own text), `in_small_co`, `in_np_income`, `in_sft`, `in_tp`, `in_tds_other`, `in_tds_nr`, `in_np_short`, `in_export`.
- C7 in_x_downstream: FIXED. Split into `in_x_fi_indirect` ("held by an Indian company or LLP that itself has foreign investment", asked only when `in_x_fdi` is no) and `in_x_ds_invest` ("did it invest in another Indian company or LLP", asked only when there is direct or indirect foreign investment). Form DI (`in_x_form_di`) needs both.
- C7 in_senior: FIXED (see B2: only the age is asked; business income is `in_business`).
- C8 in_remit no vs in_tds_nr: FIXED. `in_tds_nr` is hidden when `in_remit` is no.
- C8 in_x_fdi no vs in_x_downstream: FIXED (see C7: the downstream question is hidden when there is no foreign investment, direct or indirect).
- C8 in_export vs in_x_exporter: NOT FIXED (help only). Hiding `in_x_exporter` behind `in_export` puts `in_export` and its four GST gate questions into every foreign-trade-only check, because `relevantQuestions()` adds every question a show condition reads; that breaks the owner's "only relevant questions" rule. `in_x_exporter` help now says to answer yes if `in_export` was yes.
- C8 in_turnover <=1cr vs in_gst_2cr/5cr: DID NOT HOLD. GST "aggregate turnover" (CGST Act s.2(6), read in the 2017 text on gstcouncil.gov.in: "aggregate value of all taxable supplies ... exempt supplies, exports ... of persons having the same Permanent Account Number, to be computed on all India basis") is not the income-tax turnover of the business: a person with a business under Rs 1 crore can have professional receipts or other registrations that take GST turnover over Rs 2 crore. Hiding would also add the income-tax turnover question to GST-only checks.
- M6: FIXED. `in_x_fdi` worded for a firm ("shares in the company, or a capital contribution in the LLP or firm"); `in_x_food`, `in_x_prepack`, `in_x_iec`, `in_x_exporter` are asked of an individual only when `in_business` is yes; `in_gst_5cr` reads "Was your turnover for the year more than Rs 5 crore?".
- M7: FIXED. `in_x_g_passport`: "Do you, or a child of yours, hold an Indian passport?".
- M10: FIXED. `in_property` -> `in_property_buy` (new id): "Have you bought land or a building for Rs 50 lakh or more this financial year, or are you about to?"; Form 26QB follows it.
